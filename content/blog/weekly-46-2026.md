-  The Mountains

    It was a long drive -- over 12 hours door-to-door, with maybe about 2 hours of
    break included -- with Sid, Shreya and their parents. The kids were doing fine
    in the plains, but the curves and bends got to them once we entered the
    mountains; even more so after it started getting colder and darker as the sun
    started going down.

    But, the moment we got a glimpse of the peaks just as we entered Ranikhet, we
    all let out some oohs-and-aahs and suddenly the whole drive felt worth it.

<!--list-separator-->

-  Mala, 4 dogs and a cake

    Mala, who dropped by at our place [8 weeks ago]({{< relref "weekly-38-2026" >}}) for breakfast, lives close to
    Ranikhet. We stopped at her place on the way in our 12 hour long journey, and
    she greeted us with her army of 4 super friendly dogs, and treated us to
    freshly baked cake, even if we couldn't stop for long as the kids were getting
    cranky.

    We visited her once again in a more relaxed setting and she entertained us with
    a lot of stories of herself and her dogs in the mountains. Sid and Shreya loved
    petting the dogs and seemed super comfortable being at her place.

    It was also Harsha and Lakshmi's wedding anniversary. So, she called up a
    restaurant to make sure they wouldn't shut before we got there and have our
    dinner ready for us.

    It's nice to be in a small place and to see how Mala seems to be making an
    effort to live in a community. Unlike a lot of the city dwellers who own houses
    here, Mala lives here throughout the year, and works with the local women.
    [Needles To Say More](https://needlestosaymore.com/about-us/team/?v=13b5bfe96f3e) has around 16 women who knit high quality stuff working
    with her.
