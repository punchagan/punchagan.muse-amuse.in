-  Japanese Restaurant in Bangalore

    We enjoyed a meal at Kuuraku in Bangalore, for the most part. I lost the round
    of Janken pon (Rock, Paper, Scissors), but the wife won it. They give an
    additional quantity of some beverages if you win.

    Most establishments in Bangalore now have their [names written in Kannada](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/bengaluru/shops-must-have-60-kannada-on-name-boards-by-feb-end-or-face-action-bbmp-2824158)
    prominently. But, I noticed that Kuuraku was incorrectly spelt in Kannada,
    completely changing the meaning. The first and the last "ku" were spelt the
    same, which changed the meaning from something like eat in joy to sorrows and
    joys. This bug should've been easy to spot even for people who cannot read
    Kannada if you paid attention to the different syllables in English vs similar
    looking letters in Kannada.
