-  Protests against the education minister

    I spent a lot of time this week following the happenings at the protests in
    Delhi (and other cities) against the education minister. I had a lot of fun
    looking at the creative ideas to protest by the Gen-Z. I'm a little sad all the
    creative posters, for instance, will be lost in the ephemeral feed of
    Instagram. It'd be interesting to see an archive of the posters, if anybody
    would take the time and energy to create one.

    I loved the clarity with which some of the kids expressed themselves, in this
    [video](https://youtube.com/watch?v=lNC6SdKv2I4). It was great to see some articulate Gen-Z's in the numerous protest
    related videos I consumed this week.

    My wife had some conversations with family after which she felt like they were
    talking about an entirely different protest than the one she was talking about.
    [Manisha from Newslaundry](https://youtube.com/watch?v=iPiyKwfY2yA) gave a detailed explanation of how this came to be. I
    found it insightful to see the tactics being explicitly named and called out,
    even though they all seem familiar.

    I was shocked that [pellet guns were used](https://www.thenewsminute.com/news/fourth-pellet-like-injury-victim-at-july-20-march-22-yr-old-was-delhi-police-aspirant) against the protesters in Delhi! There
    hasn't been any acknowledgement or explanation from of the security forces,
    yet.

-  In Hyderabad

    I was in Hyderabad this week to moral support my mom undergoing a small medical
    procedure. I really liked the [hospital](https://www.fernandezhospital.com/about-us)'s vibe. Most of the staff were women and
    it seemed like they were trying hard not to add additional stress and maybe
    even relieve some of the stress that comes from being in places like hospitals
    (and airports in India).

-  Cult Fit's code of conduct

    I only got one workout done, this week, apart from playing 2+ hours of
    Badminton after more than a year. I can't seem to recall the last time I
    played.

    I had to get the staff to read Cult's [Code of Conduct](https://static.cult.fit/code_of_conduct.html) to let me workout without
    my shoes. I happened to read them recently since they sent me an email
    reminder. It says that it is **recommended** to wear shoes, but not mandatory
    unless you're using the treadmills and other such equipment. I'd have been
    really mad if I had to miss the week's only workout because I was travelling
    and not carrying my shoes. Apparently, they did send some other folks home in
    the past at that center. So, posting this as a PSA, I guess.

-  Asma Khan: Stop Calling it Curry

    I loved watching [Asma Khan in conversation with Trevor Noah](https://youtube.com/watch?v=sKrKI5nAXco) and Eugene. She
    talks about everything from girls seen as a liability in South Asia to how
    hospitality industry glorifies the image of loud and abusive chefs and
    kitchens. I was inspiring to see how passionately she talked about her work and
    her women run kitchen. It's a 2 hour long conversation that is totally worth it!
