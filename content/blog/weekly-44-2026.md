-  Movies &amp; More

    We watched Sumukhi Suresh's stand-up special [Hoemonal (2026)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt14179106/). She has some
    great observations in this set, and her use of her body and actions to animate
    the scene are so memorable.

    I watched [Dorothy (2026)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt38915678/) after a recommendation from Ringo. It pokes at some
    deep questions around gender, masculinity and violence that our species
    subjects each other to, intentionally or not. While doing so, it comes with a
    generous dose of violence like you'd expect from a contemporary Tamil movie.
    Here's a summary with no spoilers, by Ringo:

    > violence, love, friendship, violence, caste hate, violence, love, gender hate,
    > violence, love, friendship, violence, woman, love love, violence, breakdown,
    > revolt, reveal, acceptance, violence, violence, denial, acceptance, love, love,
    > reveal, fin.

    I enjoyed watching [No Reservations (2007)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0481141/) with Saa, even if it was a movie she
    had already watched. It's funny how much we identify ourselves with the work we
    do. It's going to be interesting to see how all this evolves in the age of AI.
