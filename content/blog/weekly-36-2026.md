-  Doctors who treat you like a person

    We (the wife and I) happened to be at Dr. Rajat Chauhan's [clinic](https://www.drrajatchauhan.com/back-2-fitness-clinic) for a quick
    check-up. It's such a pleasure every time to meet him. This session we chatted
    about the wife's writing journey, her illustrations, self-publishing vs
    publishing with someone like Penguin, the economics of writing and making a
    living through it, AI generated books, etc.

    I wish we had more doctors like him who treated patients like people, and not
    just a heap of flesh and bones. I'd highly recommend him if you are in NCR and
    need any ortho consultations!

    Also, on that note, we finally were able to finish formatting a short novella
    that the wife has written, and were able to send it out to a potential editor
    from a publishing group. Let's see how that goes!
