-  Modi govt compared to a simulation

    Shantanu shared this [great visual companion](https://rgrier88.github.io/modi-promises/) to a paper that compares the Modi
    government's performance against simulated data generated using a weighted
    combination of data from other countries. The countries were chosen in such a
    way that the data from the past 30 years (before Modi became the PM) matches
    India's as closely as possible, and then the data for the years 2014-2020 was
    simulated.

    I will spare you any spoilers and let you look at the paper and/or
    visualizations yourself.

    I also wonder what the data for 2021-2026 would look like.
