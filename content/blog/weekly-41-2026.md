-  AI development

    I've continued to use Claude to build the art mangement CMS, this week, using
    the art gallery as a use case for things needed in it. It is taking shape quite
    well, I must say. I could avoid a lot of the page layout, etc., thanks to this
    tool! I would really have struggled to get high resolution files of everything
    exported from the reMarkable and changing paper dimensions, etc., otherwise.

    At work, in discussions with my team, more people have expressed the opinion
    that Claude can be quite useful once you have expertise in an area, and want
    "minions" that you can guide to get the code writing work done. But, it's
    detrimental to actually building that expertise in areas that one doesn't have
    it. An idea that came up was to perhaps have two phases, and deliberately shift
    phases between learning / building expertise and code generation.
