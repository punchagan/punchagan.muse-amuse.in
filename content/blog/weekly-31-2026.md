-  Replaced broken Uke string

    After months of letting the Uke remain in the cupboard with a broken string, I
    finally had the time to replace a broken string. Like all things Ukulele until

    holding up so far.

-  Finding Forrester

    We watched [Finding Forrester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finding_Forrester) this weekend on separate screens, but synced. It
    was such a heart warming story that I kept wishing that it was a true story
    when the movie ended.

<!--list-separator-->

-  Sing Geetham

    I usually catch up on my Telugu movie backlog when the wife isn't around. (I
    was 'quarantining' this week to spare her the Flu). I finally watched [Sing
    Geetham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sing_Geetham). The songs as dialogue idea was novel. It was nice to see a couple of
    Instagram/YouTube creators I watched during the COVID-19 years, in the movie.
    But, I wasn't impressed by the plot itself, as much.

<!--list-separator-->

-  Book: Ultimate Glory

    I was gifted [this book](https://books.google.co.in/books/about/Ultimate_Glory.html?id=fruOEAAAQBAJ) on the history of Ultimate a few months ago, but only
    got to reading it this week. I read about 60 pages of it, and it has been a fun
    read. I picked it up from a small pile of books that my wife put in front of
    me, also because Mukund recently gave a plug on the TIKS Frisbee group.

<!--list-separator-->

-  Delhi Ultimate fund raising for Bharat Trophy

    We are trying to send a fully funded team from Delhi to Bharat Trophy this
    year. I've been involved in an advisory capacity and helped with getting the
    coaching staff, managers in place, who are doing all the work. It has always
    been tricky to raise funds for Ultimate teams, specially so, in the post-Covid
    era. Here's the [fund raising page](https://milaap.org/fundraisers/support-delhi-ultimate-state-team) for contributing!
